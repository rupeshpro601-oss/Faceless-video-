RICKS CLIPS — VISUAL REFERENCE PACK

This pack is for studying the visual language of the public YouTube channel Ricks Clips (@ricksclips3000).

CONTENTS
- MASTER_PROMPT.txt: prompt for an AI video creator.
- reference_gallery.html: a 10-image gallery of still frames served by YouTube's image CDN. Open it while connected to the internet; the images are loaded from their original public thumbnail/frame URLs and are not embedded in this ZIP.
- SOURCES.txt: the video titles and source links for each reference frame.

IMPORTANT
The 10 images are reference stills from Ricks Clips videos, not frames from the user's uploaded video. They are included as visual study references only. The channel creator owns the original videos and artwork. Do not repost these images or copy scripts, characters, or exact scenes. Make original stories and original scene compositions using the broad visual principles.

VISUAL POINTS TO STUDY
1. Flat 2D cartoon characters with round heads, simple faces, thick dark outlines, bean-like bodies and thin limbs.
2. Characters placed inside fully built, lit 3D cartoon environments — not empty or plain backgrounds.
3. Sets that match the story: ships, streets, rooms, offices, historical locations and outdoor environments.
4. Warm highlights against cooler blue/purple shadows; soft ambient light and visible contact shadows.
5. Low, wide, smoothly gliding camera; framing changes and close-ups that support the narration.
6. Bold white captions with dark outlines, usually only a few words at a time.
7. Character expressions, poses and props communicate the story even with the sound off.

Open reference_gallery.html in a browser with internet access to view all ten references together.
